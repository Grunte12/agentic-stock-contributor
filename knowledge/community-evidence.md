# Community Evidence Behind the Playbook

Manual, read-only research on 2026-10-10 in a normal logged-in browser session (not a scraping recommendation): 8 X searches
(178 unique posts), one author's 5-month index thread and 28 quoted older posts, and timelines of 6 recurring authors.
Authors are anonymized (Author A, B, ...) and post links are omitted; this is a summary, not a source archive.

## Limits (read before using any tip)

- Anecdotes. Income, download and reject numbers are self-reported.
- Echo chamber: most "it worked" posts come from followers of one author
  (Author A), so they are not independent confirmations.
- Survivorship: people who failed rarely post.
- Thai-language X only. No Reddit, Facebook or YouTube in this pass.
- Post images (including prompt screenshots) were not downloaded.

## Self-reported numbers

Community posts quote income, download and rejection numbers. They are self-reported, unverifiable and differ by orders of
magnitude between authors; this library does not repeat them. Do not use any such number to predict acceptance or sales.

## Not adopted

- Bot uploaders that fill the portal (Author I): conflicts with manual-upload rule.
- Running agents 24/7 or overnight generation without review: conflicts with
  per-run confirmation and QC gates.
- Firefly Contributor Bonus details: not verified.
